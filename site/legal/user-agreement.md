This is the short agreement behind the checkbox *"I agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy"* shown when you **set up a hub as its owner** or **join a hub** with an invite or open registration. It is written for both people who run hubs (**operators**) and people who use them (**users**). The full texts are the [Terms of Use](./terms) and the [Privacy Policy](./privacy); the source code stays under the [MIT licence](https://github.com/TinkerDoge/FoxFleet/blob/main/LICENSE).

## For everyone

1. **You agree to the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy** (the version shown in the app). The hub records which version you accepted and when, on your account, and your device remembers it for that hub.
2. **Foxfleet is alpha software, provided as is.** No warranty; liability is limited as the Terms say.
3. **AI output can be wrong or harmful.** You are responsible for what you ask agents to do and for checking results.
4. **Third-party agents and providers are your responsibility.** Follow their terms. Do not use subscription logins or plan-restricted keys (for example ChatGPT or Claude subscriptions, or the Z.ai Coding Plan endpoint) unless the provider allows it.
5. **Screen takeover is at your own risk.**
6. **No project accounts.** The project does not host your data and cannot recover it.

## If you are an operator (you run a hub)

- You decide who gets accounts and which agents and keys are stored. **You are responsible for your users' data**, for telling them what you store and why, and for complying with the laws that apply to you (for example data-protection law).
- You must keep the hub reasonably secure: use https, keep software updated, back up the data directory, and protect API keys stored on it.
- You may not use Foxfleet to give people access to provider services in breach of those providers' terms.
- Tell your users who to contact (you) for access or deletion requests.

## If you are a user (you joined someone's hub)

- The **operator** controls your account and can see what is stored on their hub. Do not put anything on it you would not want them to see.
- Do not misuse the hub, other people's agents or desktops, or attempt to bypass limits.
- Ask the operator, not the project, to export or delete your data.

## Withdrawing

Stop using the hub and ask the operator to delete your account. Uninstalling the apps removes their local data from your device.
